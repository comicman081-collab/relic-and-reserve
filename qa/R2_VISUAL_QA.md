# R2 Visual QA

The project was launched under Xvfb with the real OpenGL renderer and the screenshot harness produced 11 PNGs in `qa/renders/`. The contact sheet `qa/contact_sheets/r2_render_evidence.png` was opened and inspected.

The inspected screens include title, workshop, market, inventory, dirty clock inspection, cleaning, disassembly, authentication, appraisal, live auction, and upgrades. The clock mesh has a recognizable housing, dial, hands, ring, and winding element. Damage markers appear in the inspection view, and the UI remains readable at 1280×720 without a fatal blank viewport.

**VISUAL_QA: PASS_FOR_CAPTURED_SCREENS**

This does not claim coverage of every ArtifactSpec or every hardware renderer.
